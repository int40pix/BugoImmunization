# User Manual & Operating Guide

**Barangay Bugo Immunization Management System**  
Operating guidelines for Administrators, Clinical Staff, and Guardians.

---

## 1. System Access & Authentication

### 1.1 Logging In
1. Open your browser and navigate to `http://localhost/login` (or the clinic server URL).
2. Enter your registered email address and password.
3. Click **Sign In**.
4. The system will automatically route you to your appropriate workspace:
   * **Administrators and Staff**: Routed to the Central Staff Dashboard (`/dashboard`).
   * **Parents / Guardians**: Routed to the Parent Portal (`/guardian/dashboard`).

### 1.2 First-Time Login (Staff & Activated Guardians)
* If your account was generated by an administrator with a temporary password, you will be redirected to `/change-temporary-password`.
* Enter your new permanent password (minimum 8 characters with numbers and symbols), confirm it, and submit.
* Once changed, full access is immediately unlocked.

### 1.3 Guardian Password Recovery
1. On the login page, click **Forgot Password?**.
2. Enter your registered email address and click **Submit Request**.
3. A recovery ticket is submitted to the Barangay Health Center administrator.
4. Contact or visit the clinic desk to have your temporary credentials re-issued.

## 2. Administrator Guide

### 2.1 Staff Account Management
* **Creating a Staff Account**:
  1. Click **Staff Management** in the sidebar.
  2. Click **Add New Staff**.
  3. Enter the staff member's full name, clinic email, phone number, and assign their role (`Nurse`, `Midwife`, or `BHW`).
  4. Provide a temporary password and click **Create Staff Account**.
* **Deactivating an Account**:
  1. On the staff list, locate the user and click **Deactivate**.
  2. Inactive accounts are immediately prevented from authenticating.

### 2.2 Processing Password Reset Requests
* Navigate to **Password Requests** in the admin menu.
* Review pending requests from verified guardians.
* Select **Approve & Generate Temporary Password** or **Reject**.

## 3. Clinical Staff Guide (Nurses, Midwives, BHWs)

### 3.1 Registering a Family & Guardian
1. Navigate to **Guardians** in the sidebar and click **Add Guardian**.
2. Enter the guardian's name, mobile number, residential address, and parental details.
3. If the guardian wishes to access the online portal, click **Activate Portal Access** and provide their initial temporary password.

### 3.2 Enrolling a Child / Patient
1. On the Guardian's profile page, click **Add Child / Dependent**.
2. Fill in the child's:
   * Given Name, Middle Name, Surname, and optional Nickname.
   * Date of Birth and Biological Sex.
   * Birth measurements: Weight (kg), Length (cm), Head Circumference (cm), Chest Circumference (cm).
   * Birth attendant type (Doctor, Nurse, Midwife) and gestational maturity (Full-term / Pre-term).
3. Click **Save Patient Record**.
4. The system assigns a `PT-XXXXXX` number and triggers the automated scheduling engine to calculate vaccine due dates.

### 3.3 Administering a Vaccine & Updating Records
1. Open the patient profile by searching their Name, ID, or scanning their QR Code.
2. In the **Vaccine Schedule** tab, locate the due vaccine dose.
3. The system highlights the recommended lot based on **First-Expired, First-Out (FEFO)** rules.
4. Click **Administer Dose**.
5. Confirm the administering health worker name, date, and any clinical notes.
6. Click **Confirm Administration**:
   * The dose is marked completed on the child's record.
   * The batch quantity in inventory is automatically decremented by 1.
   * The digital Immunization Card updates immediately.

### 3.4 Vaccine Inventory Management
1. Navigate to **Vaccine Inventory** to inspect active stock levels and expiration warnings.
2. **Receiving New Shipments**:
   * Click **Add Batch / Stock-In**.
   * Select the vaccine, type the batch/lot number, dose quantity, date received, manufacturer, and expiration date.
   * Click **Save Batch**.
3. **Archiving Expired / Depleted Batches**:
   * For expired, recalled, or depleted lots, click **Archive Lot**.
   * Select the archival reason and confirm. Archived batches are excluded from active scheduling.

## 4. Parent & Guardian Portal Guide

### 4.1 Viewing Your Children's Health Status
* The Guardian Dashboard displays cards for each of your registered children.
* Click on a child to view:
  * **Immunization Progress Bar**: Total doses received vs. scheduled.
  * **Upcoming Appointments**: Next vaccine due dates and recommended clinic visit dates.
  * **Completed Immunizations**: Date given, vaccine brand, and health center remarks.

### 4.2 Contactless Health Center Visits (QR Code)
* Every child profile displays an encrypted **Health Center QR Code**.
* Present this QR code on your mobile screen when visiting Barangay Bugo Health Center for rapid check-in at the desk.
